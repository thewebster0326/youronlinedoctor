PDP Medical Pretoria

PDP medicals in Pretoria

The medical assessment you need before a Driving Licence Testing Centre will process your Professional Driving Permit.

A Professional Driving Permit is what allows you to drive for a living rather than only for yourself, and a medical assessment is part of getting one. The Driving Licence Testing Centre will not process the application without a certificate from a registered medical practitioner confirming you are medically fit to drive professionally.

Pretoria has a large professional driving population. Freight moving through the N1 and N4 corridors, minibus taxi and bus operators across Tshwane, delivery and e-hailing drivers, and the many fleets attached to government departments and the industrial areas around Silverton and Rosslyn. For most of those drivers the medical is an errand that costs a working morning.

That is the part worth fixing. The examination itself is short. It is the waiting, and the trip to find somewhere that does them, that takes the day.

Who needs a PDP medical

A Professional Driving Permit is required for drivers who carry goods or passengers professionally, and for drivers of dangerous goods. The permit falls into categories covering goods, passengers and dangerous goods, and the medical assessment applies across them.

  • Truck and freight drivers
  • Minibus taxi, bus and shuttle drivers
  • E-hailing and metered taxi drivers
  • Delivery and courier drivers
  • Drivers of dangerous or hazardous goods
  • Fleet drivers whose employer requires a current permit

This one has to happen in person

Most of what Your Online Doctor does is remote. A driver medical is the exception, and it has to be: it includes a vision check, a hearing check and a physical examination, none of which can be done over a video call.

What the platform does is take the arranging out of it. Message us and we will confirm a time and a location rather than you phoning around. Where mobile doctor services cover your area, the assessment can be arranged where you are.

Where you apply

The medical certificate is one document in a larger application. The application itself is made at a Driving Licence Testing Centre, and Tshwane has several, including Centurion, Waltloo and Akasia.

Requirements, fees and how recent your medical certificate has to be are set by the authorities and do change, so confirm the current position with your DLTC before you go. What we can tell you with certainty is what happens at the medical.

What to bring to the assessment

  • Your identity document
  • Your current driving licence
  • Glasses or contact lenses, if you use them
  • A list of any medication you take regularly
  • Details of any chronic condition you are being treated for
  • Hearing aids, if you use them

Questions

Common questions

Can a PDP medical be done online?

No. It includes vision and hearing checks and a physical examination, so it has to be done in person. The arrangements can be made through the platform.

Where in Pretoria can I have it done?

Message us with your area and we will confirm where and when. Where mobile doctor services cover your location, it can be arranged where you are.

How long does the assessment take?

The examination itself is usually short. Allow time for the paperwork to be completed properly, because an incomplete certificate gets rejected at the DLTC.

What if I wear glasses?

Bring them. Vision is assessed with the correction you normally drive in, so turning up without your glasses makes the result meaningless.

What happens if I do not meet a criterion?

The doctor will explain which standard was not met and what, if anything, could change it. Some findings are temporary and reversible; some are not. The certificate cannot be issued where the standard is not met.
