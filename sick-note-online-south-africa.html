Guide

Can you get a sick note online in South Africa?

Short answer: yes, where it is clinically appropriate. The longer answer is worth reading, because most of the confusion is about what a certificate actually is.

A medical certificate is not an administrative receipt for having been ill. It is a registered healthcare professional putting their name, their registration and their professional judgement behind a statement that you were unfit to work. That is why it cannot simply be requested.

It is also why the question “can I get one online” has a slightly different answer than people expect. The channel is not the issue. The assessment is. If a doctor can properly assess your condition remotely, a certificate can follow. If they cannot, it should not.

What the law actually requires

South African employment law deals with sick leave through the Basic Conditions of Employment Act. In broad terms, an employer may require a medical certificate where an employee is absent for more than two consecutive days, or on more than two occasions in an eight-week period.

The Act is concerned with the certificate being issued by a person registered with a professional council and legally entitled to practise. It is not concerned with whether the consultation happened in a room or over a video call.

What has to happen before one is issued

A consultation and an assessment. The doctor needs enough information to form a view about whether you were unfit to work, and for how long. For many everyday illnesses that can be established through history and discussion.

For others it cannot. Anything that turns on physical examination findings may need you to be seen, and the doctor will say so.

Will my employer accept it?

A certificate issued by a registered healthcare professional following a consultation is a valid medical certificate. Some employers have internal policies that go further than the law requires, so if yours has a specific form or requirement, it is worth knowing that before rather than after.

What makes a certificate questionable is not the channel. It is being incomplete, or issued without an assessment.

Backdating

This is where most requests fall over. A doctor can only certify what they are satisfied of, and certifying a period before they assessed you is a clinical judgement, not a courtesy.

Sometimes it is justified and the doctor will say so. Often it is not. Asking for a backdated certificate as a favour puts a practitioner's registration at risk, which is why the answer is frequently no.

What helps the consultation go quickly

  • When your symptoms started
  • What the symptoms actually are, in your own words
  • Any medication you have already taken for it
  • Whether you have had the same thing before
  • What your work involves - some roles matter more than others
  • Any specific form your employer requires

Arranging it

Send a WhatsApp message with your area and we will confirm a time and a place. Where mobile doctor services cover your location, the assessment can be arranged where you are.

Application steps, fees and how recent a certificate must be are set by the licensing authorities and can change. Confirm the current requirements with your Driving Licence Testing Centre.

Questions

Common questions

Is an online sick note legal in South Africa?

A medical certificate issued by a registered healthcare professional following a consultation and assessment is valid. The law is concerned with who issued it, not whether the consultation was remote.

Can I get a sick note without a consultation?

No. An assessment always comes first.

How many days can a certificate cover?

That is a clinical judgement based on your condition, not a fixed number.

Can a certificate be backdated?

Only where the doctor is clinically satisfied that it is justified.
