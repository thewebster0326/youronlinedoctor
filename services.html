What We Offer

Care across many disciplines

Every service is delivered by an appropriate healthcare professional through one digital platform.

Medical

Everyday medical care, assessed and managed remotely by a doctor.

  • Online Doctor Consultations
  • Telehealth
  • Medical Assessments
  • Prescriptions, where clinically appropriate
  • Medical Certificates and Sick Notes, where appropriate
  • Specialist Referrals
  • Laboratory Test Requests

Women’s Health

Confidential consultations covering contraception and sexual health.

  • Contraception
  • Women’s Health Consultations
  • Sexual Health

Men’s Health

Discreet consultations for the concerns men most often delay raising.

  • Men’s Health Consultations
  • Sexual Health
  • Erectile dysfunction-related consultations

Sexual & Reproductive Health

Private consultations for STIs, HIV prevention and contraception.

  • STI Consultations
  • HIV Prevention and PrEP
  • Contraception

Weight & Wellness

Clinically guided weight management and lifestyle support.

  • Weight Management
  • Lifestyle and Wellness
  • Body Composition Assessments
  • Nutritional support

Mental Health

Consultations and referral into psychology services.

  • Mental Health Consultations
  • Psychology services and referrals

Other Services

Further services available through the platform and its network.

  • Acne and dermatology-related consultations
  • Hair Loss Consultations
  • Chronic Disease Support
  • Driver and PDP Medical Assessments
  • Mobile Doctor Services

All services are subject to appropriate clinical assessment, professional scope of practice and clinical suitability.

Extended Access

Healthcare designed around your life

Illness does not always happen during traditional office hours. Your Online Doctor is designed to provide convenient and extended access to healthcare, allowing patients to seek medical assistance beyond traditional doctor’s-room hours, subject to service availability and clinical suitability.
